Sheet 02 / 06 — About

About

I'm an Information Systems student at Universitas Pelita Harapan (UPH), currently completing an internship at PT. Hitachi Construction Machinery Indonesia in the Maintenance Division. My work sits between three areas I care about: turning messy operational data into dashboards people actually use, applying machine learning to predict equipment failure before it happens, and designing physical mechanisms — from a wind-powered walking robot built with no motors, to a bipedal humanoid on a Raspberry Pi. I also spend time on competitive programming, mostly to keep my problem-solving sharp.
